Before requesting an assessment

What should I include in my enquiry?

Tell us the service you need, the general location, the project stage and your main objective. You do not need to submit private documents for the initial enquiry.

Can I ask about an existing building?

Yes. Describe its current condition and what you want to review so the scope of an initial assessment can be defined.

Does downloading a guide request an assessment?

No. You can request information separately and choose whether you would like to be contacted about your project.
